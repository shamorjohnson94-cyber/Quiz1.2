🐍 Snakes – Silent Survivors

Snakes are fascinating reptiles known for their flexibility, stealth, and survival skills. They live in forests, deserts, grasslands, and even water. Most snakes are solitary and rely on camouflage and patience to hunt.

  • 🐍 No legs — movement through powerful muscles
  • 🌡️ Cold-blooded and energy efficient
  • 👀 Some detect heat to find prey
  • 🛡️ Important for ecosystem balance
